## Route A — The big swing: Philadelphia, July 4 🎆

**Make July 4 your second great romantic anchor of the trip** (Moulin Rouge being the first). A Round of 16 World Cup match *and* America's 250th birthday, in the city where it all started.

The shape of it — turn Jul 3–5 into a Philly weekend (all three are TBD):

- **Fri Jul 3** — Drive up after work / morning. Check into a center-city hotel. Easy first night: cheesesteaks done properly, a walk around Old City / Elfreth's Alley.
- **Sat Jul 4** — Daytime: Independence Hall, the Liberty Bell, the 250th festivities (this will be the biggest July 4 in the country — expect huge crowds and security). Late afternoon: **head to the match (5pm kickoff)**. Evening: fireworks over the Parkway.
- **Sun Jul 5** — Slow brunch, Reading Terminal Market, Rittenhouse Square stroll, drive home relaxed.

**Reality check on this one:** it's the marquee match on the marquee day, so it's the hardest and priciest ticket of any option here (see budget below), and the city will be at maximum capacity. If you want this, **book tickets and a hotel early** — both will move fast. If live tickets don't pan out, you still keep the whole Philly + fireworks weekend and just watch the match at a fan zone or bar in the city. Win either way.

## Route B — The easier live match: NY/NJ (MetLife)

MetLife hosts the most matches of any stadium. Two land on your TBD days:

- **Round of 32 — Tue Jun 30, 5pm ET**
- **Round of 16 — Sun Jul 5, 4pm ET**

The **Jul 5 Round of 16** is the better pick — weekend timing, a higher-stakes game, and you can wrap it in a romantic NYC weekend (Jul 3–6 are all TBD). Train or drive (~4 hrs / Amtrak ~3.5 hrs from DC), stay in Manhattan, do a show / rooftop / Central Park, bus or train out to the stadium for the match.

The **Jun 30 Round of 32** works too if you'd rather keep early-July free, but it's a weeknight and a lower-stakes round.

This route is generally **easier to get into and cheaper than the Jul 4 Philly match**, while still being a live World Cup knockout game.

## Route C — Stay near DC: watch-party dates 🍻

Lowest cost, lowest logistics, still a blast — and easy to slot into the *weekday* TBD days (Jun 29, Jul 1, Jul 2, Jul 6, Jul 7) without committing a whole weekend.

**Free / fan-zone:**
- **FIFA Fan Zone — National Mall** (Jun 11 – Jul 19): live screenings of every knockout game, food, music, exhibits. Free.
- **Hi-Lawn atop Union Market**: turf pitch, giant LED screens, ~1,000 fans, DJs. Reservations available, free admission.
- **D.C. United "United in Play"**: the city's official FIFA watch party, family-friendly.

**Soccer bars:**
- **Hall Pass (Chinatown)** — billed as "Global Matchday HQ," every match, country-themed parties, international beers + street food.
- **Lucky Bar (Dupont)** — DC's longtime soccer bar.
- **Elephant & Castle (Downtown)**, **Metrobar (Edgewood, 20-ft screen)**, **Sport & Social (U Street)**.
- **Tom's Watch Bar** — National Harbor & Navy Yard locations, wall-to-wall screens.

A nice romantic version: pick one big knockout match, grab a good outdoor spot (Hi-Lawn or Metrobar), make it a relaxed evening date — drinks, food, the crowd energy — then a walk after, in the same spirit as the rest of the itinerary.

## 🎟️ Tickets & budget

| Option | Realistic cost (2 people) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Face value (Supporter tier)** | ~$60/ticket + 15% FIFA fee | Cheapest, but knockout supporter tickets are sold via FIFA draws and largely gone by now for casual buyers. |
| **Resale — NY/NJ match (Route B)** | Round of 16 resale from ~$525/ticket, avg ~$1,658 | Generally more available than Philly. |
| **Resale — Philly Jul 4 (Route A)** | Top end of (and likely above) the ~$1,658 R16 average | Marquee match + marquee date = scarcest, priciest. |
| **Bar / fan zone (Route C)** | $0 (fan zone) to ~$100–$150 for a nice bar evening | Best value, zero ticket risk. |

**Where to buy (live match):** use **FIFA's official ticket portal and the official FIFA Resale Marketplace** (FIFA.com/tickets) first; **StubHub / SeatGeek / SeatPick** as backups. Avoid social-media sellers, classified ads, and anyone asking for wire transfer or crypto.

**Budget guidance:** Routes A/B are real money once you add tickets + hotel + travel (plan **$1,500–$4,000+** for a live-match weekend for two). Route C is essentially free-to-cheap. A sensible play: **try for the Jul 4 Philly tickets, set a price ceiling, and if it's blown past it, pivot to the Philly weekend + watch the match at a fan zone — or just do a great DC watch-party date.**

## ✅ Recommendation

1. **Anchor it on July 4 in Philadelphia.** It's the once-in-a-lifetime overlap (World Cup R16 + US 250th) and it fills three blank days beautifully. Decide a ticket budget now and start watching resale.
2. **Keep Route C as the safety net** for the same weekend and for filling a couple of weeknight TBD days cheaply — the National Mall fan zone and Hi-Lawn are genuinely great date spots.
3. **Skip the Final** (Jul 19) — it conflicts with Momo's Grand Marais birthday, which should stay the priority.

When the **bracket is finalized (after the group stage ends Jun 27)** you'll know *which teams* play in Philly on Jul 4 and at MetLife on Jul 5 — that's the moment to pull the trigger on tickets.
